Generated by `python -m logokit reconstruct owlkei` from `construction.py` (the approved plan) with decisions D1–D6 at their defaults:

## How to reproduce

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt   # numpy, Pillow, SciPy (compare only)
python3 -m logokit reconstruct owlkei                  # writes the SVG, prints per-piece deviation (stdlib only)
python3 -m logokit check owlkei                        # reads the SVG back: node counts, tangent breaks
.venv/bin/python -m logokit compare owlkei --scale 4   # analysis/compare/*.png and the metrics below
```

- **Total:** 100 nodes, against 202 in the original. That includes 7 circular arcs and 8 straight lines.
- **Shared edges:** wherever two shapes meet, they use the same curve objects (brow / head, left pupil / head, body / beak). No seam can open between them.
- **Smoothness:** `check_svg.py` finds 0 tangent breaks. Every turn of more than 1° is one of the 40 intended sharp corners (tips, notches, the spike, the beak and stripe points). The softest corner is 14°, at the brow tip, the same as in the original.

## Measured accuracy

**Raster, at 4 px per unit.** Each pixel is unmixed into navy, gold and white coverage, so anti-aliased edges count for the color they come from:

| Layer | IoU | Edge distance, median | p99 | p99.9 | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Navy | 99.57% | 0.25 units | 1.00 | 1.82 | 4.47 |
| Gold | 98.31% | 0.25 units | 1.35 | 1.95 | 2.57 |

- **Edge distance:** each boundary pixel of one image, measured to the nearest boundary of the other, in both directions.
- **Median 0.25 units:** this is one pixel at this scale, the resolution of the measurement. At the original 1408 px width it is 0.06 px.
- **Outliers above 1.4 units:** they form only four clusters, and each one is a deliberate change listed below:
  - the beak's right point (2.6),
  - the right pupil's top edge and notch tips (2.2),
  - the brow tip, where two single pixels on a hair-thin point reach 4.5.

**Vector, measured as the maximum distance from the original outline to the new one:**

- **Béziers:** at most 1.24 units, and at most 0.9 for 31 of the 33 pieces.
- **Arcs:** at most 1.83 units, except the right pupil next to its notch (2.23, see below).

At 512 px and smaller sizes (`sizes.png`), the reconstruction and the reference can't be told apart.

## Measurement fixes

- **Color classification:** the first classifier matched each pixel to its nearest color. That turned every 50% navy-on-white edge pixel into gold, which drew a false gold outline around all navy shapes. Gold IoU read 95% instead of 98.4%. Unmixing fixed this.

## Corrections to the geometry plan

- **Chin bowl:** the bowl is not one circle. One circle deviates by 3.9 units. The lower part (original segments 7–12) is an arc with center (2997.0, 1512.5) and r 445.4 in source units. The upper-left part bends to a larger radius with no tangent circle joint, so it is a Bézier.
- **Left blade tip:** this is not a circle (it deviates by 2.1 units at the inner side), so it is a Bézier.
- **Right eye white, lower right:** past (3290, 1540) the trace runs 1.5 units inside the circle. The body follows it with a Bézier from (3198, 1649) on.
